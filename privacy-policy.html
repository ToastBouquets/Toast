1. Introduction & Scope

Toast Bouquets LLC (“Toast Bouquets,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a floral, gift, wedding, funeral, and event-service business based in Gastonia, North Carolina. We create custom bouquets, arrangements, gift baskets, and event flowers, and we offer pickup, local delivery, online quote requests, and consultations.

This Privacy Policy applies to our website at toastbouquets.com, our online “Get a Quote” form, and the ways we communicate with you by email, phone, and text message, as well as information we receive when you contact us through social media or our business listings. Our order and service terms are covered separately in our Terms of Service.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when you request a quote, place an order, or contact us. This includes your name, phone number, email address, and preferred contact method, along with details about what you’re looking for:

  • Type of service and the occasion (bouquet, gift basket, wedding, funeral, event, etc.)
  • Requested date
  • Budget
  • Venue or delivery location
  • Floral preferences, colors, style notes, and any special instructions
  • Any inspiration photos you choose to share

You are responsible for having the right to share any photos or content you send us.

3. Delivery & Recipient Information

To arrange pickup or local delivery, we may collect the recipient’s name, delivery address, phone number, and any delivery instructions or card message you provide. See Section 13 regarding information about other people.

4. Payment Information

Payments, deposits, and invoices are processed securely by our third-party payment processor, Square. Your payment card details are provided directly to Square and handled under Square’s own privacy and security practices. We do not store full payment-card numbers on our systems; we generally receive only limited confirmation details such as the amount, date, and a partial card reference.

5. Information Collected Automatically & Cookies

When you visit our website, basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type, device type, and pages viewed) may be collected automatically to help the site work, stay secure, and load properly. Our site may use essential cookies or similar technologies for these purposes. We do not currently run third-party analytics tools or advertising/remarketing pixels on our website. You can manage cookies through your browser settings, though disabling some may affect how the site functions.

6. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and prepare quotes
  • Plan, create, and fulfill your orders, including weddings, funerals, and events
  • Arrange pickup and local delivery
  • Communicate with you about an active quote or order through your preferred method
  • Process payments and keep accurate business records
  • Operate, secure, and improve our website and services
  • Comply with legal obligations and prevent fraud

Submitting a quote request allows us to contact you about that request. We use your information to serve you — not to sell it.

7. Calls, Texts & Emails

When you submit our quote form and check the consent box, or otherwise give us your phone number, you agree to receive calls, text messages (SMS), and emails from Toast Bouquets about your request and order. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply.

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, and you can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in the email or by contacting us. Opting out of promotional messages does not stop essential messages about an active quote or order.

8. How We Share Information

We do not sell your information. We share it only as needed to run our business, with:

  • Service providers who help us operate — including Square (payments), Vercel (website hosting), and GoHighLevel (our quote form, customer records, and messaging tools). These providers process information only to perform services for us.
  • Staff and contractors — such as delivery help, designers, or event staff — who need limited details (like a delivery address or design notes) to complete your order.
  • Legal and safety purposes — when reasonably necessary to comply with the law, enforce our agreements, or protect the rights, property, or safety of Toast Bouquets, our customers, or others.

9. We Do Not Sell Your Information

Toast Bouquets does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it for third-party targeted advertising. The information you give us is used only to provide our products and services to you.

10. Data Retention

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, communicate with you, maintain business and financial records, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete it or remove identifying details.

11. Information Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information we hold. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You share information with us at your own risk.

12. Your Privacy Choices & Rights

You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to request that we delete it, subject to applicable law and reasonable verification of your identity. We may need to keep some information to complete an order, maintain records, or meet legal obligations. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights under state law; to exercise any of these, please contact us using Section 17.

13. Information About Gift Recipients & Others

When you order a gift or delivery, you may provide information about another person, such as a recipient’s name, address, and phone number. By providing this information, you confirm you have permission to share it with us for the purpose of fulfilling the order. We use recipient information only to complete delivery and related communications.

14. Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can remove it.

15. Third-Party Websites & Platforms

Our website and messages may link to or rely on third-party services we do not control, such as Square, social media networks, and mapping services. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third parties, and we encourage you to review their own privacy policies.

16. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we do, we will update the “Last Updated” date above. Your continued use of our website or services after an update indicates your awareness of the revised policy.
